Getting started

How it works

  1. 01

    Request a quote

    Send photos of your cards and tell us what you'd like fixed. No commitment yet.

  2. 02

    Get your quote

    We look over the photos and reply — usually within 1 day — with options and pricing.

  3. 03

    Send your cards

    Drop them off in North San Jose, or mail them in from anywhere in the US.

  4. 04

    Cards come home

    We restore them, share before-and-after photos, and return them the way they came.
